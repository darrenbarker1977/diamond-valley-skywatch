Compass & mounting tips

Upcoming stops
    How this mission adapts

    Sky checks run every minute while the app is visible. Fresh weather, a new observing location, a different planning time or changes to your eyepieces rebuild the upcoming stops. Your current target stays selected when recommendations change.

    Targets need suitable forecast hours, darkness, a useful altitude and safe separation from the Sun. The Moon can rule out faint targets. These are model forecasts, not measurements of cloud, seeing or transparency. Confirm actual conditions outdoors.

    Offline missions use the last downloaded forecast and show its age. A new location or a night outside the downloaded hours needs a connection for weather. The sky planner can still calculate positions offline.
