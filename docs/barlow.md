# SVBONY 2× Barlow

The owner’s confirmed kit contains three eyepieces (20 mm and 12.5 mm 70° wide-angles and the SVBONY SV135 7–21 mm zoom) plus a separate SVBONY nominal 2× 1.25-inch Barlow. The Barlow is owned and enabled for recommendations by default; saved version-2 kits gain it once. Availability can be disabled without changing eyepieces or telescope focal length.

At the default 1200 mm telescope focal length, the combinations give approximately 120× with the 20 mm, 192× with the 12.5 mm, and 114–343× over the zoom’s 21–7 mm range. Actual Barlow magnification depends on optical spacing. The guide never recommends stacking Barlows and caps beginner recommendations at 220×. Large or low-altitude objects stay at low power. Finding and alignment use a bare eyepiece; the normal 20 mm / 60× start is preserved. Bare setups win equal-power comparisons; higher-power Barlow options are conditional on a sharp, steady view.

The equipment disclosure lists accessories separately from eyepieces. For use, fit the complete Barlow in the focuser adapter and put one eyepiece into it, then refocus. Fitting instructions describe the complete nominal-2× assembly, not an unscrewed lens cell with a different multiplier.
